Settings

    

      
Local language model
Content is sent exclusively to your local Ollama service.

      
What should the AI look for?
This search brief supplements the system prompt. For example: “Look for personal projects, old photo archives, and wallet files.”

      
System prompt
The core brief for assessments. Read-only access and safety boundaries are enforced in application code.

      
Investigation depth & budgets
Progressive rounds include discovery, selective reading and AI evaluation in the time budget. Continue deepening grants a new budget and reuses evidence. The folder limit applies to legacy sampled investigations.

      
Legacy detail mode & Obsidian
Disable for a structural vault with complete fingerprints, file/folder links and exact duplicate groups. AI topic links require this option. Personal data is reviewed first; recognized operating systems, applications, dependencies and caches are grouped without content reads by default.
Explicit all-file mode: fingerprint and inspect program/system files as well. This can take hours or days. Grouping never means files are safe to delete.
Exports always contain filenames and assessments. Excerpts are off by default; enabling them can include sensitive source text. Each completed detail investigation creates a separate vault in local app storage; your existing Obsidian vault is not modified.

      
Filters & content
Without image analysis, only image metadata is reviewed. PDF and Office excerpts are limited; unreadable content is identified.
Excluded paths are logged, but their contents are not inventoried. Patterns match names and relative paths.

Delete this investigation?
This permanently removes this investigation’s local catalog, findings, saved report and in-app knowledge graph. This cannot be undone.
Your source files and exported Obsidian vault folders will be kept. Reports you downloaded are also kept.
The investigation will stop after the current file read or AI request finishes. It will then be deleted and the queue will continue.


Eject source drive?
macOS will safely eject the external drive containing this source, including its other volumes. Saved investigations and reports remain available.
Pause the running investigation first and wait until it shows Paused. Then open Eject drive again. No drive can be ejected while an investigation is active.
Internal disks are protected. If another app is using the drive, macOS may refuse to eject it.
